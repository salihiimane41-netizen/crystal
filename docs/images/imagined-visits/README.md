# Imagined star visits

All material here belongs to an explicitly fictional school-project restaurant concept. No scene is evidence of a real celebrity visit, real preference, quotation, collaboration, or endorsement.

- `restaurant-photo-corner.png`: original AI-generated reference interior, based on the site's existing concept venue. Shared landmarks are the CHAWARMA L7ILWIN neon, cream plaster, red-tiled counter, blue lamp, wall vinyl, plants, and yellow table.
- `nancy-ajram-imagined-visit.png`: generated scene of Nancy Ajram with a takeaway bag.
- `maher-zain-imagined-visit.png`: generated scene of Maher Zain seated with Syrian food.
- `elgrande-toto-imagined-visit.png`: generated scene of ElGrande Toto at the counter with a wrap.
- `charles-leclerc-imagined-visit.png`: generated scene of Charles Leclerc beside an original fictional chef.
- `justin-bieber-editorial-background.png`: a copy of the original empty interior used under a name-led editorial poster. **No photograph, portrait, or likeness of Justin Bieber is depicted.** The image tool declined that photorealistic request, so this card uses the brief's editorial fallback instead.

The generated photo scenes include an “IMAGINED VISIT / AI CONCEPT” border; the website also labels every card and detail panel. Text captions and imaginary food preferences were written for the project, not attributed as celebrity quotes.
